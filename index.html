Saving and continuing later

3 quick steps so you never lose your work

This page does not save automatically. If you close or refresh the tab, your work is gone, unless you saved a file first.

  1. 1

    Save your work

    Tap Save at the top of the page. A file downloads to your phone or computer.

  2. 2

    Keep the file safe

    Leave it in Downloads, or send a copy to yourself on WhatsApp, email or Google Drive.

  3. 3

    Continue later

    Open this page again, tap Open and choose that file. Everything comes back as you left it.

What is the file? It ends in .json and works like a save game. It holds your teams, logos, fixtures, scores and settings. Only this app can read it, so there is no need to open it in anything else.

Tip: save again after you change teams or fixtures. Each save is a new file with today's date, so older ones stay as backups.
